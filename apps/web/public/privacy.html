Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Brackify helps people create, manage, and share tournaments. This policy explains the information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, username, profile details, and authentication provider. When you use Brackify, we store tournament information you create or join, including tournament names, rosters, teams, brackets, scores, invitation codes, and optional images.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve Brackify, authenticate accounts, synchronize tournament updates across web and mobile, show the content you choose to share with tournament participants, prevent misuse, and respond to support requests.

Sharing and visibility

Tournament organizers choose whether a tournament is public or private. Information in a public tournament may be visible to people who access it. Private tournament information is shared with the organizer and people the organizer invites or who join with a valid invite code. Please do not add sensitive personal information to tournament names, rosters, or notes.

Service providers

Brackify uses Firebase to provide authentication and database services, Cloudinary to host images that organizers upload, and Vercel to host the web app. These providers process data as needed to provide their services. If ads are enabled in the future, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own policies and any consent choices presented to you.

Data retention and deletion

We keep account and tournament data while it is needed to operate Brackify. Organizers can remove tournaments, teams, and players in the app. You may request account-related assistance or deletion by contacting us.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials and private invite codes confidential.

Children

Brackify is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Brackify evolves. We will post the updated version here and change the effective date.
